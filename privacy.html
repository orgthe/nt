Privacy Policy

We're a small, high-touch studio — we only ask for what we need to grow your brand, and we never sell it. Here's exactly what we collect and why.

Last updated: October 3, 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you submit our discovery form or message us, we collect the details you choose to share: your name, work/brand email, social handle, selected plan, budget range, and any notes about your brand goals.

If you become a client, we also store the raw footage, brand assets, and account access you provide so we can produce and schedule your content.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your details solely to respond to your enquiry, prepare proposals, deliver the content services you've signed up for, and keep you updated on your monthly calendar.

We may send occasional service-related messages over email or WhatsApp. We will not add you to unrelated marketing lists without your consent.

3. Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Your data is shared only with the members of our studio working on your account and the trusted tools we use to deliver work (for example, cloud storage and scheduling platforms).

We may disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights, but never for advertising resale.

4. Content & Portfolio Use

Published content we create for you may be featured in our portfolio or case studies. If you'd prefer your work stay private, just tell us and we'll keep it off our public channels.

5. Data Retention & Security

We retain enquiry details for as long as needed to follow up, and client project files for the duration of our engagement plus a reasonable archival period. You can request deletion at any time.

We apply sensible safeguards to protect your information, though no method of transmission over the internet is ever fully secure.

6. Your Rights

You can ask to access, correct, or delete the information we hold about you. Email us at contact@nanbateam.com and we'll action it promptly.

7. Contact Us

Questions about this policy? Reach our studio at contact@nanbateam.com or +91 93638 82411. We're available Mon – Sat, 10:00 AM – 7:00 PM IST.
